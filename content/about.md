*"The role of the artist is to make the revolution irresistible."* - Toni Cade Bambara

*"What we cannot imagine cannot come into being."* - Bell Hooks

*"Hope is not passive. Hope is not blah, blah, blah. Hope is telling the truth. Hope is taking action. And hope always comes from the people."* - Greta Thunberg 

At age 16, Natasha co-founded **Fridays for Future** Exeter with her friend Sam in their sixth form college cafeteria. They joined the global Youth Strike 4 Climate movement led by Greta Thunberg and organised within the national UK Student Climate network. The local movement in Devon, brought thousands to the streets of Exeter and culminated in the biggest peaceful protest the South West has ever seen with three and half thousand young people shutting down Exeter High Street in September 2019. It created a movement of movements led by the next generation, featured on ITV Westcountry, BBC Spotlight, the Guardian and more. It now lives on as a community that has formed into others responding to the needs of the city and across the South West…and the world! 

Since then, Natasha has organised with a myriad of different groups across the climate movement for over six years. From taking to the streets at COP26 in Glasgow and the G7 Summit in Falmouth with **Extinction Rebellion** to lobbying for the UKs first ever student written Bill, the Climate Education Emergency Act, in Parliament with **Teach the Future**. Her activism spans advocacy, education, political organsing and direct action. From creating spaces for young people to explore eco anxiety with **Force of Nature** and the **Climate Psychology Alliance** while also disrupting high-profile events and challenging political leaders with **Green New Deal Rising**, pushing climate justice and the cost of living into the media spotlight.

Natasha graduated with **First-Class Honours in BA (Hons) Theatre and Social Change from Rose Bruford College** in July 2024, becoming part of the program's inaugural cohort. During her time there, she led local campaigns such as **Plant Based Universities** and **People & Planet’s Divest Borders** and **Fossil Free** movements with which she was successful in campaigning for Rose Bruford becoming the first specialist drama school in the UK to reject fossil fuel funding.

Following this, Natasha took up her role as **Artist for Change: Climate Emergency** at the **Theatre Royal Plymouth** (supported by the Jerwood Developing Artists Fund) successfully advocating for the theatre to sign oil sponsorship-free pledge and declare a climate emergency with Culture Declares.

Natasha is also a founding member and national coordinator of the **Theatre Green Captains UK**, a network of students and young theatre makers formed in collaboration with the **ABTT Sustainability Committee** in response to the Theatre Green Book. The Green Captains regularly contribute to Green Book committees and initiatives, fulfilling the need to put young people at the forefront of pioneering climate conscious theatre. Recently, Natasha spoke to industry leaders at the launch of the Theatre Green Book V.2  at the National Theatre: "As a young theatre maker, I'm not dreaming of sitting in the artistic directors chair but simply ensuring my generation and the generations to come can grow up in a world where it's possible to even make theatre and make it responsibly...." 

Her debut show, **The Planet Is___**, co-written and performed with Meg McGrady, premiered at **Theatre Deli’s Shift+Space** festival and **The Other Palace’s Musical Bites Spring Season**. Extracts were also performed as a curtain raiser for Sarah Middleton’s Shewolves at **Southwark Playhouse**. The two-person verbatim musical explores friendship, climate justice, and the right to protest in the UK through the lens of two youth climate activists over the course of a year.

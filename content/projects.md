# Coffee Shop Revolution

A short one woman play published as part of **Fore Street Stories** project in partnership with **Bookbag** and **Crankhouse Coffee**, under the mentorship of **Davina Quinlivan**. 

Bookbags Fore Street Stories project is a local initiative funded by **Creative Arc** hosting **young writers in residence** and pairing them with independent businesses on Fore Street in Exeter culminating in an anthology of creative writing that tells the stories of Fore Street. 

Coffee Shop Revolution by Natasha Pavey is the third piece in the series and follows a young activist organising to build a revolution from her laptop in a coffee shop, unbeknownst that the revolution is already taking shape around her within the small business’ and communities created on Fore Street, Exeter. A short play about hope, connection and home. 

Read more about the project featured in the BBC Spotlight [here](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5ym4drkydjo). 
Pick up a copy of Fore Street Stories in partnering stores on Fore Street, Exeter or drop us a line to request a copy digitally!

# It's an EMERGENCY!

A site specific outdoor dance piece and short film by Natasha Pavey made in collaboration with **Theatre Royal Plymouth** (as their Artist for Change - Climate Emergency), **Earth Minutes** and **Culture Declares** with support from **Jerwood Developing Artists Fund** and mentorship from **Chloe Naldrett** and the **Julie's Bicycle’s** Creative Climate Accelerator programme.

The piece integrates a soundscape of voices of local artists, activists and climate campaigners alongside movement co-created and performed by an ensemble of local dance artists in key locations in Plymouth. It marks the Theatre Royal’s Climate Emergency declaration by interrogating the everyday ‘business as usual’ response to the climate emergency and inspiring action in the local community.

The film premiered at Plymouth Arts Cinema in July 2024.

Watch the full short film [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ARnJFe1_VXs). 
Watch the behind the scenes and overview of Natasha’s year in residence at the Theatre Royal [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ToH2J9guq6A).

# The Planet Is___

An original verbatim musical by Natasha Pavey and Meg McGrady about friendship, the right to protest and climate justice documenting a year of UK politics. 

Performed as part of the **Theatre Deli** Shift + Space Festival 2024, **The Other Palace** Musical Bites Summer Season 2024, and as curtain raiser for Sarah Middleton’s Shewolves at **Southwark Playhouse** in June 2023, the show is also a Jane Goodman Award Winner.

Created with mentorship from **Brian Lobel, Yvette Nolan and Bryony Kimmings**, the show documents a year in British protest history, using a soundscape of voices of youth climate activists in the UK mixed with movement and song to shine a light on criminalisation of peaceful protest. 

Watch the trailer [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WFeF14V00rc).

# G7: The Freedom Fighters

Whilst studying at drama school in London, I skipped lectures for a week to camp in Falmouth whilst the G7, a group of leaders from the world’s richest countries, met in their annual meeting to coordinate economic policy. The Freedom Fighters is a short film I created for an assignment documenting the protests, people and campaigners that joined together in the South West to force world leaders to prioritise climate action. 

Watch [here](https://youtu.be/bkPjBqN5Bng).
